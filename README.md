# Vendor Maps

A free list that helps event organizers find vendors: businesses seen on public exhibitor and
vendor lists at fairs, festivals, markets, expos and trade shows, sorted by category, place and
reach (local, state, regional or national).

- Free for organizers. Business names only; no contact people, emails or phone numbers published.
- Contact is opt-in, with Vendor Maps passing an organizer's message to vendors likely to be interested.
- We're not selling your data.

The site is `index.html`, reading `data/vendors.json`. Links can open it already filtered:
`?state=OH`, `?cat=<category key>`, `?reach=local|state|regional|national` and `?q=<words>`. How the list is built and how to rerun it:
[pipeline/README.md](pipeline/README.md). Where it is thin and what's queued next:
[docs/COVERAGE.md](docs/COVERAGE.md).
