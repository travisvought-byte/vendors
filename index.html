How the list is built

Every business here was named on a public list: an event's own exhibitor, vendor or sponsor page, or one of our source-checked directories. Each listing keeps the events it was seen at and the date it was last checked.

Reach is worked out from where a business shows up. Local means one city or metro area, state means across one state, regional means two or three states, and national means the business serves the whole country or turns up in four or more states.

When an event lists a maker only by their own name, we count them but don't list them.
